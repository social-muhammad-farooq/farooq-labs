Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Farooq Labs ("we", "us") develops apps for Atlassian Cloud products such as Jira and Confluence. Privacy questions can be sent to social.muhammad.farooq@gmail.com.

2. Data our apps process

Our apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run on Atlassian's infrastructure. Customer content such as Jira issues or Confluence pages is processed within Atlassian's platform to provide the app's features. Unless an app's Marketplace listing explicitly says otherwise, we do not copy, export or store customer content on our own servers.

We may receive limited information needed to operate and support the apps: Atlassian site identifiers, license information from Atlassian Marketplace, and the details you voluntarily send us in support emails.

We use this information only to provide, secure and improve our apps and to respond to support requests. We do not sell personal data and do not use customer content for advertising.

5. Sharing

We share data only with Atlassian as the platform provider, and where required by law. Any future subprocessors will be listed on this page.

6. Retention and deletion

Data held by Forge is removed under Atlassian's app-uninstall and data-deletion processes. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. You may ask us to delete data we hold about you at any time.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes

We will update this page and its date when this policy changes.
